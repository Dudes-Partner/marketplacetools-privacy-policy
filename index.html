1. Introduction

MarketplaceTools is a Chrome extension that helps marketplace sellers analyze and improve their product listings. When you view a product page on a supported marketplace, MarketplaceTools reads the listing, checks it against listing-quality guidelines, and shows the results and improvement suggestions in Chrome's side panel. The currently supported marketplace is OTTO (otto.de). MarketplaceTools may support additional marketplaces in future releases.

MarketplaceTools is provided by Dudes6Partner - Willi Deubner ("we", "us", "our"). MarketplaceTools is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, operated by or endorsed by OTTO or any other marketplace.

This policy explains what information MarketplaceTools handles, where that information is kept, who receives it, and what we do, and do not do, with it.

In short: The listing analysis runs locally in your browser. If you sign in, we process your account data to provide account features, and your target keywords and Action Plan progress can be synchronized to your account. If you ask for AI suggestions, the listing text needed for them is sent through our server to our AI provider. Analytics and advertising trackers are not used, and we do not sell your data.

2. Information MarketplaceTools accesses

Product pages on supported marketplaces

MarketplaceTools works only on product pages of supported marketplaces, which is currently OTTO (otto.de). When you view a product page there, MarketplaceTools reads the listing information shown on that page:

  • page address, product ID and variant ID
  • title, brand, category and breadcrumb navigation
  • current price and comparison (crossed-out) price
  • Bulletpoints, Besondere Merkmale and product description
  • image addresses and image descriptions (alt text), and the number of videos
  • average rating and number of reviews (not the review texts or reviewer names)
  • delivery information and availability
  • test seals and sustainability seals (labels and image addresses)
  • Rich Content text and image addresses

MarketplaceTools uses this information to analyze the listing and to provide its features.

Reading and analyzing this information happens locally in your browser. It does not mean the information is sent anywhere. Only cloud synchronization (section 5) and AI suggestions (section 6) send selected information, as described there.

MarketplaceTools does not read your marketplace account, login details, passwords, cookies or payment information, and it does not change marketplace pages.

Other websites

MarketplaceTools does not run on other websites and does not read their content. Chrome lets MarketplaceTools see the address of a browser tab only when that tab shows a supported marketplace; for other websites, it does not see the address at all (see section 9). MarketplaceTools does not collect your browsing history and does not keep a record of the websites you visit.

Information you enter

You can enter or add information yourself:

  • target keywords for a product
  • your Action Plan progress (which recommendations you selected and marked as done)
  • competitor products you add, with your own notes and tags
  • search positions (rank tracking) that you record by hand or import from a CSV or JSON file you choose
  • your email address and password when you sign in (section 4)

Files you import are read locally and are not uploaded.

4. Account and authentication data

You can use the listing analysis without an account. Signing in is needed for cloud synchronization and AI suggestions. Accounts are created by MarketplaceTools; you cannot sign up inside the extension.

When you sign in, the extension sends your email address and password to our authentication provider, Supabase, which checks them. MarketplaceTools does not store your password; Supabase stores only a protected (hashed) form of it. As part of providing its service, Supabase also processes technical information such as your IP address.

After you sign in, Supabase issues a session. The extension stores it in your browser's storage and renews it automatically, so you stay signed in after closing the side panel or restarting Chrome. If the session can no longer be renewed, you are signed out and asked to sign in again.

Resetting your password: if you forget your password, you can ask for a recovery code on the sign-in form. Supabase sends the code to your email address. You enter the code and a new password in the extension, and Supabase stores the new password in protected (hashed) form. The temporary session used for the reset is kept only in memory and is not saved. After the reset, all sessions of your account are signed out, and you sign in again with the new password.

Signing out ends the session in this browser and removes it from your browser's storage. Your local work stays on your device, and your synchronized data stays in your account.

For each account, we keep:

  • the account record at Supabase: your email address, user ID, and when the account was created and last used
  • an access profile: your user ID, your access level, and when the profile was created and changed

We use this information to sign you in and to decide which features your account may use. Access levels are set by MarketplaceTools.

5. Cloud synchronization

When you are signed in and your account has access to cloud synchronization, MarketplaceTools keeps a copy of part of your saved work in your account, so that you can use it on other devices. For each product that has a product ID, only the following is stored:

  • the marketplace and the marketplace's product ID
  • your target keywords, and whether they were checked
  • your Action Plan selection and the recommendations you marked as done (with the time you marked them)
  • when you last changed this data

Not synchronized: listing texts (title, Bulletpoints, Besondere Merkmale, description, Rich Content), prices, images and image addresses, page addresses, analysis results, view settings, competitor data and rank-tracking data. These stay on your device.

The synchronized copy is stored with Supabase and linked to your user ID. Database rules ensure that each account can access only its own synchronized data, and only while the account has access.

Your browser's copy is the working copy:

  • Changes are uploaded shortly after you make them.
  • The synchronized copy is downloaded when you open a product.
  • If the same product was changed on two devices, the most recent change is kept.
  • The first account that synchronizes in a browser is linked to that browser's saved workspaces. If another account signs in on the same browser, those workspaces are not uploaded to it or downloaded from it.

Deleting synchronized data: if you clear a saved workspace while signed in, its synchronized copy is deleted as well. A deletion marker is kept so that your other devices also remove the workspace. It contains only the marketplace, the product ID and the time of deletion, not your keywords or Action Plan.

6. AI processing

MarketplaceTools can suggest improved listing text using an AI model. This happens only when you explicitly request it: you must be signed in, your account must have access, and you must ask MarketplaceTools to generate suggestions.

When you request an AI-generated improvement, the extension sends the following to the MarketplaceTools server, together with your sign-in token:

  • the listing's title, Bulletpoints, Besondere Merkmale and description
  • Rich Content teaser headline, teaser text and footnote
  • brand, category and breadcrumb navigation
  • your target keywords, with their status and priority
  • which sections should be improved, the improvement goals, length limits and the language
  • a random request number, used to match the answer to the request

Not sent: the page address, product and variant ID, prices, images and image addresses, rating, review count, delivery information, availability, number of videos, seals, Rich Content images and highlight blocks.

The MarketplaceTools server checks your sign-in and access level and applies usage limits (section 7). It then sends the listing information and keywords to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to generate the suggestions. Anthropic does not receive your email address, user ID, sign-in token or IP address from us. Anthropic processes this information to provide the service, according to the applicable service agreement and its privacy terms. The extension never contacts Anthropic directly.

The suggestions are returned through the MarketplaceTools server to your browser. Our server does not store the listing text, the request sent to Anthropic, or the suggestions. In the extension, the suggestions are kept only in memory while the side panel is open. You decide whether to copy them; you can also save them as a file on your device.

7. AI usage records

For each AI request, the MarketplaceTools server records:

  • your user ID
  • the time of the request
  • the AI model used
  • the amount of text processed (input and output tokens)
  • the outcome (for example: success, timeout, or no usable result)

The server stores these records in our database at Supabase, in an area that the extension and user accounts cannot access.

These records contain no listing text, keywords, prompts, AI suggestions, email address or IP address. We use them to apply fair-use limits per minute and per day, and to manage costs. Our server's error logs record only the type of event (for example "rate limited"), not user IDs or content.

Retention periods for these records are determined by MarketplaceTools' operational requirements and applicable legal obligations.

8. Analytics, cookies and tracking

MarketplaceTools does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising trackers, advertising identifiers or any other tracking tools. The extension does not set cookies.

10. Network requests

Listing analysis

MarketplaceTools does not send listing data to us for the analysis. To show the product's main image in the side panel, your browser loads it directly from the marketplace's servers (currently OTTO), the same image you see on the product page. As with any image your browser loads, the marketplace receives the usual technical information (such as your IP address) with that request.

Sign-in and cloud synchronization

When you sign in, the extension connects to Supabase to sign you in and to renew your session. If you reset your password, it connects to Supabase to request the recovery code and to set the new password. While you are signed in, it also connects to Supabase to upload and download the synchronized data described in section 5.

AI suggestions

Only when you request AI suggestions, the extension connects to the MarketplaceTools server, which connects to Anthropic as described in section 6. The extension never connects to Anthropic directly.

All of these connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Without signing in, MarketplaceTools sends no data to us, to Supabase or to Anthropic.

This privacy-policy web page

The web page that shows this policy loads its layout styles (Bootstrap) from cdn.jsdelivr.net and its icons (Font Awesome) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. When you open the page, your browser connects to those servers, which receive the usual technical information such as your IP address. The page itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics. This concerns only the web page, not the MarketplaceTools extension.

12. Data sharing

  • Listing analysis: runs in your browser. No listing content is sent to us.
  • Cloud synchronization: only the selected workspace information described in section 5 is stored in your account.
  • AI suggestions: only the listing content described in section 6 is sent, and only when you request AI suggestions.
  • Usage records: limited information about each AI request is recorded to manage AI usage (section 7).

We share information only with the service providers listed in section 11, and only as needed to provide MarketplaceTools. We do not:

  • sell or rent personal information or other user data
  • share user data for advertising
  • show targeted advertising
  • track you across websites
  • use advertising cookies
  • collect your browsing history
  • use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending

If you contact us (see section 20), we use your message only to answer your request.

13. Limited Use

MarketplaceTools' use of information it handles, including the marketplace website content it reads, complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MarketplaceTools uses this information only to provide the features described in this policy: analyzing product listings, synchronizing your keywords and Action Plan, and generating listing suggestions when you request them. It transfers information only to the service providers named in section 11, and only to provide these features. It does not use the information for advertising or to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. Our server does not store your listing text, so no one at MarketplaceTools reads it.

14. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the extension.

Delete it in MarketplaceTools. Under More (German: Mehr), you can delete your saved data at any time. It is removed from your browser's storage straight away.

  • Saved workspace → Clear saved workspace (one product) or Clear all saved OTTO workspaces (German: Gespeicherten Arbeitsstand löschen / Alle gespeicherten OTTO-Arbeitsstände löschen)
  • Competitor Intelligence → Remove (one competitor) or Clear Competitor Data (German: Entfernen / Wettbewerber-Daten löschen)
  • Rank Tracking → Remove (one keyword, with its recorded positions) or Clear Rank Data (German: Entfernen / Ranking-Daten löschen)

Remove the extension. When you remove MarketplaceTools from Chrome (for example on the chrome://extensions page), Chrome deletes all data that MarketplaceTools stored in your browser. This includes the guided-tour and language settings, which cannot be deleted separately inside the extension.

Clearing Chrome's browsing data (history, cache, cookies) does not delete your saved MarketplaceTools work. It may reset the language setting.

Sign-in session: signing out removes your session from your browser.

Synchronized data: clearing a saved workspace while signed in also deletes its synchronized copy (section 5). Synchronized data is otherwise kept as long as your account exists. Deletion markers are kept so that your other devices also remove deleted workspaces.

Account data: you may request deletion of your MarketplaceTools account and the associated cloud data by contacting us using the privacy contact in section 20.

AI usage records: these records are deleted when your account is deleted. Otherwise, retention periods for these records are determined by MarketplaceTools' operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. You can contact us about these records (section 16).

AI requests and suggestions are not stored on our server.

Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal retention obligations.

15. Data security

  • Data used only for the local analysis stays in your browser and is not transmitted.
  • The extension's storage is restricted to the extension's own pages, and the part of the extension that runs on marketplace pages does not read or write it.
  • The extension loads no code from the internet, and imported files are processed locally.
  • Connections to Supabase and to the MarketplaceTools server are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules allow each account to access only its own synchronized data.
  • Our server accepts AI requests only from the MarketplaceTools extension and only with a valid sign-in from an account with access, and it applies usage limits. Provider credentials, such as the AI provider's key, are kept on our server, never in the extension.

Data saved in your browser is protected by your device and your Chrome profile. Anyone with access to your computer and Chrome profile may be able to see it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the listing analysis without an account.
  • You decide whether to sign in and whether to request AI suggestions.
  • You can delete your local data, sign out, and clear synchronized workspaces at any time (section 14).
  • You can remove the extension at any time, which deletes its local data.

If data protection law such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to request restriction of processing, to object to processing, and to data portability, where the legal requirements are met. These rights apply to your account data, your synchronized data and your AI usage records. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To make a request, contact us (section 20).

Data used only for the local analysis stays in your browser, so we do not hold it.

17. Children's privacy

MarketplaceTools is a tool for business and professional marketplace sellers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

18. International data processing

When you sign in, use cloud synchronization or request AI suggestions, some of our service providers may process information outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Anthropic, for example, is based in the United States. Where this happens, we take the steps required by applicable data protection law to protect your information.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MarketplaceTools changes, for example when we add features or support for another marketplace. Whenever a version of MarketplaceTools materially changes how it handles information, for example what information it collects, what is sent off your device, to whom or why, we will update this policy before that version is released. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
